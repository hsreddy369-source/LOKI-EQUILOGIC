Rule 1 • Form & Class 5/6 Bounce-Back

Rewards 1–2–3 finishes in last 3 runs. In Class 5 & 6, forgives a single older '0' when followed by a recent bounce-back (1–4) or class drop, while penalizing multi-zero (00) slumps (-24 pts).

Rule 2 • Course & Distance (CD / C / D)

Prioritizes proven CD winners (+18 pts), with moderate positives for C or D (+8 pts). Exempts lightly-raced (≤4 starts) Class 5/6 handicap debutants from C&D penalties.

Rule 3 & Rule 4 • Going & Penalty-King Days

Cross-checks winning ground and 7–35d peak fitness. In Class 5 & 6, last-time-out winners turning out within 1–10 days under a fixed penalty earn a +26 to +34 Penalty-King Bonus instead of a quick-backup penalty.

Rule 5 & Rule 6 • Weight/OR & First-Time Gear

Evaluates OR vs. last winning mark and first-time h1, b1, v1, p1, t1. In Class 5 & 6, apprentice/conditional claims ((3), (5), (7)) and featherweights (≤8-12 / ≤53.5kg) earn up to +16 pts.

Rule 7 & Rule 8 • Draw & Furlong Transition

Standardizes all distances to Furlongs (1m = 8f). Triggers Stepping Up ("running on strongly") and Dropping Back ("weakened / faded") tactics starting at ±1.0f, and relaxes sprint draw penalties over 9.5f+ trips.

Rule 9 • Race Class Regime (Class 1 to Class 6)

Strict Conditional Logic & Standardization Specification

Every runner uploaded or fetched into EquiLogic AI is evaluated deterministically against all 9 rules and Race Class (Class 1–6) regimes below:

1. Form Rule (Last 3 Runs)

Awards positive score to horses with recent finishes of 1, 2, or 3. Penalizes horses with a '0' (unplaced) or letters F (Fell), U (Unseated), or P (Pulled Up) in their last three runs.

2. Course & Distance Rule

Prioritizes horses with 'CD' (Course & Distance winner) as a top Green Flag. Treats 'C' (Course winner) or 'D' (Distance winner) as moderate positives.

3. The Going Rule

Cross-references today's track condition (Good, Soft, Heavy, Standard, etc.) with the horse's historical winning/placing conditions. Flags horses that have never placed on today's ground as an Unproven Ground Risk.

4. Days Since Last Run Rule

Ideal racing fitness window is 7 to 35 days. Absences over 150 days are flagged as "Returning from a layoff" and cross-checked against historical fresh performance.

5. Weight & Official Rating (OR) Rule

In handicap races, compares current OR against last winning OR and evaluates physical weight burden. Significant OR hikes (+5 lbs or more) or heavy top-weights flag speed compromise risks.

6. First-Time Headgear Rule

Detects first-time Hood (h1), Blinkers (b1), Visor (v1), Cheekpieces (p1), or Tongue-tie (t1) as a high-impact variable for sudden improvement.

7. The Draw Rule (Flat Racing Only)

Evaluates starting stall number against course-specific geometry (e.g., low stall 1–4 advantage on sharp turning circuits like Chester, Kempton, Wolverhampton, and Hyderabad Malakpet).

8. Distance Standardization & Transition Rule

Converts all distances to Furlongs (1 Mile = 8f, 200m = 1f). Evaluates Same Distance (≤1f delta + Top 3 last time), Stepping Up (≥1f longer: Unproven Stamina Risk vs. "running on strongly / finishing well" catalyst), and Dropping Back (≥1f shorter: Early Pace Risk vs. "weakened / faded / tired" tactical drop).

9. Race Class & Class 5 / Class 6 Specialist Regime Rule (Class 1 – Class 6)

Dynamically adapts scoring weights across Class 1 (Group/Grade/Listed), Class 2 (Premier/Heritage), Class 3–4 (Mid-Tier), and Class 5–6 (0–75 / 0–60 Low-Grade & Basement Handicaps):
• Class 5 & 6 Quick-Backup Penalty-King: Last-time-out winners turning out within 1–10 days under a fixed penalty earn +26 to +34 pts (beating official handicapper reassessment) instead of a quick-backup or OR-hike penalty.
• Class 5 & 6 Unexposed Handicap Improver: Lightly-raced runners (≤4 starts or 2nd-start handicap/nursery improvers) with progressive top-3 form earn +24 pts over exposed multi-season basement horses.
• Class 5 & 6 Bounce-Back & Class-Dropper Relief: Forgives a single older unplaced '0' when followed by recent top-4 recovery or a drop into calmer Class 5/6 waters (+18 pts), while penalizing multiple consecutive zeros (00, -24 pts).
• Class 5 & 6 Apprentice Claim & Class Ceiling: Rewards 3 lb / 5 lb / 7 lb claiming riders and featherweights (+6 to +16 pts) plus top-of-the-grade OR ceiling leaders (+10 pts).
